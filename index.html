How to shortlist an item and check QC

  1. Save the exact option. Record the listing URL, color and size. Compare garment measurements with an item you already own. For shoes, ask whether the chart lists foot length or insole length.
  2. Check ordering and returns. Open the CAFANS listing and confirm which shopping agents are available for that item. Ask about seller returns and the time window before placing an order.
  3. Review warehouse photos if available. Compare the variant, label and measurements against your shortlist. Ask for a close-up of any seam, mark or measurement you need to verify before approving shipment.
  4. Compare shipping quotes for the same parcel. Check destination, packed dimensions, billable weight and what each quote includes.

OrientDig spreadsheet FAQ

What is an OrientDig spreadsheet?

It is a shopping shortlist that keeps product links, variants, measurements and prices together. This independent guide links to CAFANS listings and gives you a checklist for evaluating them.

Can I order every item here through OrientDig?

Agent availability can vary by item and destination. Check the options on the CAFANS product page, then confirm availability with your agent.

Where can I see quality-control photos?

Ask your shopping agent whether warehouse photos are available for your order and whether you can request additional photos or measurements before shipping.

Do the catalog prices include shipping?

Catalog prices are listed item estimates. Use the agent’s current checkout amounts and shipping quote to estimate what you will pay.
